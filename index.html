Quick Notes transforms your raw text, topics, or handwritten note photos into beautifully structured documents — ready to download as PDF or Word and share with teammates instantly.

Features
Smart Formatting
Type or paste any text — instantly converted into polished, organized notes with no AI needed.
Multiple Image Upload
Upload multiple handwritten note photos — all images embedded and organized in the document.
AI Web Research
Enable "AI Enhance" to call GPT-4o and enrich notes with related topics. Graceful fallback if it fails.
PDF & Word Export
Download polished notes as a multi-page PDF or editable Word document instantly.
Merge & Convert PDF
Merge multiple PDFs or convert any number of images into a single PDF file.
Share with File
Share generated PDFs directly via WhatsApp or Gmail — with the actual file attached.
